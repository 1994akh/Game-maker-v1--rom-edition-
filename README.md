# Game-maker-v1--rom-edition-

Design repo for a Golden Sun × Pokémon game: a native Android app (Galaxy Z Fold 7) built in Godot. The engine
lives in the private repo `GameMaker-Engine`. (The repo name predates the move away from a ROM hack.)

- **Start here:** [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md), with its 14 Oct header of what changed, and [docs/CHANGELOG.md](docs/CHANGELOG.md).
- **The story:** [docs/story/story.md](docs/story/story.md), written 14 Oct against [docs/story/BRIEF.md](docs/story/BRIEF.md). The story chat is archived (16 Oct); open story questions sit in [docs/story/for-the-story-chat.md](docs/story/for-the-story-chat.md) and are answered on the decision page. Raw material in [docs/story/drafts/](docs/story/drafts/README.md).

- **Plans awaiting decisions:** [docs/design/progression.md](docs/design/progression.md) (levels, power scaling, evolution), [docs/design/art-integration.md](docs/design/art-integration.md) (the new libraries and 64-px art), [docs/design/items-and-economy.md](docs/design/items-and-economy.md) (items, shops, drops, catching, coins), [docs/design/equipment.md](docs/design/equipment.md) (gear and its screens), [docs/design/menus-and-navigation.md](docs/design/menus-and-navigation.md) (menus, pause, settings, saves, maps, compass), [docs/design/adult-mode.md](docs/design/adult-mode.md) (the Adult-o-Vision glasses), [docs/design/day-night-clock.md](docs/design/day-night-clock.md) (the day/night clock), [docs/design/demo-plan.md](docs/design/demo-plan.md) (the Act I demo: build order and its questions), [docs/engine/resources/needed-assets-2026-10-16.md](docs/engine/resources/needed-assets-2026-10-16.md) (what art and sound the game still needs), [docs/engine/resources/credits.md](docs/engine/resources/credits.md) (who made what we use).
