# Halcyra: story draft ("Elemental epic")

Draft for review. Sections 1–6 are story decisions, which you gave me. Section 7 lists everything that needs a mechanic or changes how the game plays, each with my recommendation. All names are invented placeholders. "Weyard form" is DESIGN.md's term for the regional forms.

## 1. Logline and themes

Six Wells once fed the world of Halcyra. Now they are sealed, and the world's edges are pouring into the void. Arlen is the sworn keeper of the Earth Well. He hunts Corvin, a friend everyone thought had drowned, who has come back to break the seals. Then both crews read the same ledger and learn that the seals are siphons. Every seal Arlen guarded was draining the edges to keep the capital alive.

Themes: duty against truth; two sides that are each half right; found family across a divide; legendaries that are wounded, not wicked.

## 2. The world

**Shape.** Halcyra is a flat ocean world. Its rim spills away as **the Rimfalls**, which creep inward every year.
- **The Spine** is a north–south continental wall. It splits the **Brightwater** (east, where the game starts) from the **Duskwater** (west, the Rimborn heartland).
- **The Galeway** is an east–west storm band. It runs between two windless belts where leviathans hunt (**the Stillwaters**), and it cuts both seas into Upper and Lower halves.
- **Layers:** **the Cloudreach** (a cloud sea), **the Deep Shelf** (the sea floor) and **the Hollow** (Giratina's mirror world).

**The six Wells.** Each is stoppered by a **Wardstone**.

| Group | Well | Location | Legendary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Earth | The Marrow Vault | Marrow Isle, the home island | Groudon |
| Flame | The Forgeheart | Embral Isle | Ho-Oh |
| Forest | Eldergrove Heart | The Galeway | Celebi |
| Sea | Silverdeep | The Deep Shelf | Lugia |
| Sky | Stormspire | The Cloudreach | Rayquaza |
| Shadow | The Umbral Gate | The north-west rim | Giratina |

**The threat.** The official history says Adept wars set the Wells raging 600 years ago, and the first Wardens sealed them. The truth comes out at the midpoint. The Wardstones don't stop the flow. They send it up the Spine into **the Crown**, a lattice under the capital that keeps the heartland green while the rim starves and falls. It was meant to last one generation and was never undone. The legendaries have been drained for six centuries, which is why each one wakes in a rage.

**Factions**

| Faction | Leaders | What they want | Why they're morally grey |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tidewardens**, a naval order ruling from **the Pinnacle** on top of the Spine | **Lord Warden Aurelian Vey**, who knows the truth; **Commodore Ilse Graymantle**, Arlen's patron, who doesn't | Keep the seals. Vey's last resort is to wake Groudon with the Red Orb and raise the Spine: the centre survives, the rim drowns | Millions on the Spine really do live off the Crown |
| **Rimborn**, refugees from the falling rim | **Captain Sael**, Corvin's foster mother (the moderates); **Hask** (the **Breakers**) | Open the Wells: Sael one at a time, Hask all six at once | Hask's way releases 600 years of force at once and drowns the Spine |
| **Free sailors**: the Tradewind Bazaar, pirates, monasteries | — | Profit and neutrality | They sell to both sides |

The third way the crews find: open each Well once its legendary is calmed, then break the Crown so the flow returns gradually.

## 4. The opening

**Mission 1, "The Keeping Night" (Arlen alone, Marrow Isle)**
- **Setting:** Stonehollow's festival, which honours the sealing.
- **What happens:** a quake sends Arlen, the Vault keeper's apprentice, into the Marrow Vault's upper halls. He solves Move, Pound and Scoop puzzles and takes his first partner from the keeper's hatchery.
- **The rival:** at the inner door, which opens only to two Earth Adepts working together, a masked intruder is waiting. It is Corvin, Arlen's childhood friend, lost at sea five years ago. Corvin asks for help ("that door hides the only way to save my home"). Arlen's oath says never.
- **The clash:** a normal battle, Adept and partner against Adept and partner. The defeat rule stays as decided: if Arlen loses, he wakes in the village and tries again.
- **How it ends:** two Earth Adepts fighting at the seal stirs Groudon in its sleep, and the hall splits. Corvin escapes down the underground river. Commodore Ilse, in port for the festival, pulls Arlen out, names Corvin a seal-breaker and deputizes Arlen to hunt him.

**Mission 2, "The Drowned Boy" (played as Corvin, alone)**
- **Route:** through Marrow's sea caves (Earth puzzles, river currents) to a Rimborn skiff, then to **Fallhaven**, a cliff town on the eastern rim.
- **What the player learns:**
  - Sael pulled Corvin from a wreck when he was fourteen.
  - Fallhaven loses a street a month to the Rimfalls, and Tidewarden patrols turn refugee boats away from the Spine.
  - A rubbing from the Forgeheart says the Wells were "stoppered to feed the Crown".
  - Corvin came home for a second Earth Adept, because a legend puts an ancient ship behind that door.
- **Set piece:** a cliff gives way, and Corvin clears escape paths with Move and Pound while families run.
- **The turn:** Hask offers to tear out all six Wardstones at once. Corvin refuses and sails for the Flame Well.
- **Why his side is sympathetic:** he isn't after power, the people he's saving are on screen, and the only villain in sight is the edge of the world.

- Earth and Sea Psynergy, which every crew has;

- items already on the critical path.

Puzzles for other groups only guard optional rewards.

This all fits WORLD_PROPOSAL §6:
- Waterfalls climb north.
- Underwater maps line up with the surface maps above them.
- No more than about 10 movable objects per screen.
- One tileset per world layer.
- Every unlock is a story flag.

## 7. Mechanics questions

1. **Crew-against-crew battles (mission 6).**
   - **Recommendation:** the enemy crew uses its real Adepts at their current levels, with a fixed story set of Pokémon. Nothing carries back afterwards.
   - **Why:** your other crew is neither punished nor exploitable.
2. **Bag and coins before the crews unite.**
   - **Recommendation:** each crew has its own bag and coins until mission 11, then they merge. Pokémon storage is shared throughout.
   - **Why:** rival crews sharing key items makes no sense in the story, and it keeps each crew's gating clean. This changes the *proposed* shared-bag rule, for Act I only.
3. **Temporary loss of an Adept (Tamsin, missions 6–7).**
   - **Recommendation:** allow it. She keeps her Set Pokémon and comes back at full health.
   - **Why:** real stakes at small cost.
4. **Travel before the Wayfarer.**
   - **Recommendation:** pick point-to-point routes on a sea chart (ferry, cutter or skiff). Open sailing starts at mission 11.
   - **Why:** the story picks the crew until then, and the freedom arrives together with crew swapping.
5. **Walking on the world map.**
   - **Recommendation:** yes, Golden Sun style, on the big islands (Marrow, Embral, the Spine coast, Dunesea). Small islands are entered straight from the ship.
   - **Why:** it gives the Golden Sun feel. The GBA cost the proposal warned about mostly falls away on native.
6. **Both-crews dungeons (missions 10, 11, 18 and 22–24).**
   - **Recommendation:** both crews are on site. Relay stones (restoration points inside the dungeon) switch control, and one crew's progress opens paths for the other.
   - **Why:** it reuses the handover rule and gives The Lost Age's split-party feel.
7. **Missions that need a named Adept, after swapping unlocks.**
   - **Recommendation:** a "Needs X" mission requires X in the active crew, and the home island prompts you. All other missions work with any crew.
   - **Why:** homecomings stay coherent, and crews stay free.
8. **How legendaries join.**
   - **Recommendation:** calming a legendary unlocks its group's top Summon at once. The legendary itself joins as a Set Pokémon after the ending, at its Well.
   - **Why:** a Set Ho-Oh at mission 4 would break balance, and this keeps the payoff.
9. **Time travel (Celebi, mission 14).**
   - **Recommendation:** two versions of the grove maps, switched at Celebi shrines. Changes made in the past persist into the present.
   - **Why:** it is map variants by flag, with no new system.
10. **Sky layer.**
    - **Recommendation:** one small sailable cloud-sea area with wind-current tiles and a few sky isles.
    - **Why:** variety without building a second world.
11. **Undersea layer.**
    - **Recommendation:** Dive maps on foot from mission 17. After Lugia, the ship dives at marked spots onto a Deep Shelf of one or two map chunks.
    - **Why:** it answers DESIGN.md's open question and adds a second way across the Spine.
12. **Ship upgrades from opened Wells.**
    - **Recommendation:** yes:
      - Flame: engine (crosses the Stillwaters)
      - Forest: living hull (sails up rivers)
      - Sea: bubble hull (dives)
      - Sky: wings (hops reefs, reaches the Cloudreach)
      - Shadow: fog sight (shows hidden isles)
    - **Why:** every site reward opens more of the map.
13. **Permanent world changes.**
    - **Recommendation:** Fallhaven is replaced by a "fallen" map after mission 10, and rim islands visibly regrow after the ending. The collapse has no timer, only scripted crumbles between areas.
    - **Why:** it shows the stakes without the frustration of timers on touch controls.
14. **Mirror world.**
    - **Recommendation:** yes. Recoloured copies of 5–6 earlier maps, with new puzzles.
    - **Why:** cheap content and a Golden Sun-style final dungeon.
15. **Minecarts (mission 23).**
    - **Recommendation:** scripted rides. Pound the switch points to choose the track.
    - **Why:** very little engine work.
16. **Final battle relay (mission 24).**
    - **Recommendation:** Vey fights in two phases, one per crew, and the player picks the order. His HP carries over, and a loss sends both crews back to the last relay stone.
    - **Why:** the two-crew theme pays off at the end.
17. **Post-game.**
    - **Recommendation:** the legendaries join (see question 8). Fog-sight isles hold superbosses and the remaining Weyard starters as Djinn-style finds.
    - **Why:** it reuses systems that already exist.
