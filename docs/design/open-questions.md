# Open questions (swept 18 Oct)

## 1. What this is

The story chat is archived and will not be used again, so every question and decision now lives on the decision
page. This page holds what was still left to that chat, or still open in the design docs, after the 190 answered
decisions, the demo plan's 40 among them: placeholders with no decision behind them, rules still marked proposed, and
points the docs leave "open", "later" or "for you to say". Each item names the page it came from. None of them
blocks the work in flight; the ones that touch a later slice say which.
